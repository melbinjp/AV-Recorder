    How to use AV Recorder

    1. Pick what to record: Camera, Screen, Screen + Cam or Audio.
    2. Start the preview and check your framing and the microphone level.
    3. Press the red button. A countdown gives you time to get ready.
    4. Press it again to stop. Download it, share it, or find it later under Your recordings.

    Good to know

    • Your work is safe. Recordings are written to this device every second. If the browser crashes or the tab closes, reopen this page and the recording will be in Your recordings.
    • Recording the screen works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on computers. Phones and tablets don't allow it in a browser, so use your phone's built-in screen recorder instead.
    • Sound from your computer: Chrome and Edge can record a tab's sound (and the whole system's on Windows and ChromeOS). Tick Share audio in the sharing dialog. Firefox and Safari record only your microphone.
    • While recording another window, use Pop out (Chrome and Edge) to keep the timer, pause and stop buttons, and your teleprompter, floating on top.
    • File formats: MP4 opens in every editor and phone. When your browser can only make WebM, YouTube still accepts it directly.
    • On iPhone and iPad, keep the app open while recording. iOS pauses the camera when you switch apps, so the recording pauses too and resumes when you come back. Use Share → Save Video to put it in Photos.
    • Install it for one-tap access that also works offline: use Install app, or on iPhone Share → Add to Home Screen.

    Private by design: there are no accounts, uploads or trackers. Your recordings never leave this device unless you download or share them.
